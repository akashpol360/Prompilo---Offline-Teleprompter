Prompilo Privacy Policy

Prompilo stores scripts in a local database on your device. Videos you record are saved locally in Movies/Prompilo and in a private recovery folder on your device.

Camera access is used to show a live preview and capture video. Microphone access is used to capture audio during recording. Prompilo does not upload your scripts, camera feed, microphone audio, or recordings.

Free users can choose to view a rewarded ad to unlock one video recording. This build uses Google's test ad unit. If production ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads may collect and use device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location as described in Google's privacy disclosures. Google Play processes purchases for the yearly, monthly, and one-time Pro plans; Prompilo receives purchase status to unlock Pro. Prompilo does not create an account or sync scripts to a cloud service.

If you choose to send feedback, share Prompilo, rate the app, or share a recording, the selected email, sharing, or Play Store app handles that action. Please review that recipient's privacy practices.

Deleting a script removes it from the local script database. Delete videos separately through your device's gallery or file manager. Removing a video draft deletes its private recovery copy.

This policy applies to the Prompilo Android application. Last updated October 19, 2026.
